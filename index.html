Train Your Mark

Record the sound you will use as the mark. Whistle, clap, snap, tone, clicker, spoken word, or real sticks. The app listens and builds a print (waveform bands). That print is the mark: when Listen hears it, sticks slam and the take is logged.

Tap Record → Yes → countdown 5…0 → Make Noise now. Five samples. Mic stays warm through the countdown.

Test the mark

Arm Test Listen, make the sound once. MATCH means the print is ready. NO MATCH means record again on Train.
