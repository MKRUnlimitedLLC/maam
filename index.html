Train Your Mark

Record the sound you will use as the mark. Whistle, clap, snap, tone, clicker, spoken word, or real sticks. The app listens and builds a print (waveform bands). That print is the mark: when Listen hears it, sticks slam and the take is logged.

Tap Record → ready → countdown → make the sound at RECORD. Five samples.

Test the mark

Arm Test Listen, make the sound once. MATCH means the print is ready. NO MATCH means record again on Train.

Board

On set: fill the board, tap Listen, hold up to camera, make the mark. When the print is heard, sticks slam, flash fires, and the take is logged. Mark and sticks still work if Listen misses. Ads never appear on the board face. Dummy slate — wall clock, not LTC.

Train mark sound

Record the same sound 5 times — a whistle, a snap, a clicker, a spoken “mark,” or your sticks. Tap Record → ready prompt → countdown 5…1 → mic records ~1s. Fingerprints only. Match strictness is under More → Match threshold.
